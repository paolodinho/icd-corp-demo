Who we are

Partnering for clean cities and sustainable industry

Vietnam Industrial and Urban Development JSC (ICD) is one of the leading companies in manufacturing and supplying environmental sanitation equipment and urban equipment. The company has carried out many projects serving communities, organizations and businesses at home and abroad.

We supply products and professional consulting to local governments, investors and contractors in designing and building waste management systems, pollution control and environmental protection.

Capabilities

In control of every step, from design to warranty

ICD does more than sell equipment. We advise, manufacture, import, store, install and service it ourselves, so quality, schedule and cost stay under control on every project.

01

Consulting and solution design

Site surveys of real operating conditions, equipment configuration and custom design for each factory, urban area and project.

02

Manufacturing and custom fabrication

ICD Waste Technology and ICD Cabin Technology workshops build cabins, guard booths, toilets and specialized electric vehicles on integrated machinery, to the size, material and color ordered.

03

Genuine imported equipment

Distribution of scrubber dryers, vacuum cleaners, sweepers and sweeper vehicles from Fiorentini, Tenax, Glutton, Magnum and EP, with full CO and CQ documents.

04

Warehouses and nationwide delivery

Warehouses in Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City keep large stock ready, with transport and crane unloading to the site.

05

Showroom and test runs

Equipment is displayed and test-run so customers can try it before deciding, and ICD regularly appears at industrial fairs and exhibitions.

06

Installation, handover and warranty

Technicians install, train operators and provide genuine 12-24 month warranty with nationwide technical support.

How we work

A professional process, built around the customer

  1. 01

    Survey and consulting

    Receive requirements, survey the site and define the operating problem.

  2. 02

    Design and quotation

    Propose options, drawings, technical specifications and a transparent quotation.

  3. 03

    Manufacture or import

    Build in our workshops or import genuine equipment, with quality checks before delivery.

  4. 04

    Installation and handover

    Transport, install, test run, and train the operators.

  5. 05

    Warranty and maintenance

    Genuine warranty, periodic servicing, spare parts and technical support.
