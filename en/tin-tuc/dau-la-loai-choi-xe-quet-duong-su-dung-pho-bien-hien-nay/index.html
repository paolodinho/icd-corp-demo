What is a street sweeper brush? What are the most commonly used types today? Find out more in the article below.

The street sweeper brush is an indispensable component in modern street sweeping and vacuum trucks. They come in a wide variety of types, including plastic strip brushes, steel strip brushes, and brushes with a mix of plastic and steel strips. Depending on your needs, you can choose the appropriate street sweeper brush for the road surface to be cleaned.

Structure of an industrial street sweeper

A street sweeper is an industrial device that has been widely researched and manufactured in recent years. Since its introduction, the industrial street sweeper has received widespread support from the public due to its convenience and ability to quickly clean waste and dust.

Street sweepers feature an automatic control system

These street sweepers have an automatic control system and modern features, allowing them to operate on various terrains. The equipment on the vehicle has strong suction power, and the sweeper brush operates continuously, making it easy to collect waste into the hopper, even in the hardest-to-clean areas.

In our country, many types of cleaning and sweeping machines are currently in use, such as Eureka, Palada, Isuzu, Fiorentini, etc. These industrial street sweeper brands consistently receive positive reviews from consumers due to their quality and operational performance. All these machines are manufactured using modern processes and equipped with advanced technology, ensuring durable and long-lasting performance.

Main structure of a street sweeper

Generally, each sweeper model has its own function and operation method. However, the structure of industrial street sweepers shares the following common features:

  • Handlebar: This is considered the basic component that any vehicle must have. The handlebar helps the user easily control the vehicle on every road, ensuring superior cleaning capability.
  • Seat: An important part for the driver to adjust and perform road cleaning functions most effectively. Typically, each vehicle manufacturer designs the seat with different materials.
  • Wheel: The part that makes the movement of the street sweeper easier and smoother.
  • Accelerator pedal: The part used to adjust the movement speed of the vehicle at different locations.
  • Emergency stop button: Some types of garbage sweepers are equipped with an emergency button. This feature is used to stop the wheel or sweeping brush when hitting an obstacle that could damage the equipment and ensure safety for the driver.
  • Street sweeper brush: This is an indispensable part to bring garbage into the suction mouth. Every garbage sweeper is equipped with this part to ensure the best cleaning and sweeping of the road surface.
  • Suction mouth: After dirt and garbage are collected by the street sweeper brush, they are brought into the suction mouth. The suction mouth is responsible for bringing the garbage to the garbage container.
  • Garbage container: The place to store various types of dirt and garbage. Depending on the volume of the container, it can hold 3 cubic meters, 4 cubic meters, or 5 cubic meters.

Notes to ensure the vehicle structure operates smoothly

  • Before operating the sweeper, you should remove the air filter for the blower to ensure thorough cleaning.
  • Cleaning the vehicle’s container thoroughly helps prevent garbage from accumulating and taking up space during operation.
  • Check the engine and start a test run before heading out to perform tasks.

These are considered essential notes for the street sweeper to operate at its best performance. Make sure the vehicle structure is stable before performing any activity to ensure the safety of the driver and people moving on the road.

Street sweeper brush - The indispensable part of a vacuum street sweeper

Road sweeping brushes come in many types, including some common ones such as brushes made of plastic bristles, iron bristles, or a combination of plastic and iron bristles. Depending on the vehicle brand, they design various brush types to suit specific functions. Additionally, the choice of street sweeper brushes also depends on different work environments such as factories, schools, parks, streets, and so on.

The street sweeper brush is an indispensable part of a garbage sweeper truck.

Without this component, the environmental sanitation vehicle cannot operate properly. The processes of dust suction and waste collection would be disrupted or might not follow the correct procedure. Consequently, street cleaning efficiency would not meet expectations. Given such an important role, you should pay attention to choosing the right sweeper brush when you need to clean your living and working environment.
