Choosing booth material and size for durability, good looks and savings

Factors to consider when choosing guard booth material

Choosing the right material is critical when designing a guard booth. It directly affects durability, appearance and how well the booth works. Here are the key factors to consider.

Factors to consider for guard booth material

  • Durability and weather resistance: Booths usually stand outdoors, so the material must withstand sun, rain, wind and other environmental effects. Popular choices are stainless steel, galvanized steel, aluminium composite panel and fiberglass composite (FRP). They share strength, corrosion resistance, load bearing and heat tolerance, ensuring a long service life.
  • Sound and heat insulation: In noisy places such as beside main roads, factories or construction sites, sound insulation is essential. Heat insulation also keeps the guard comfortable in hot weather. Prefer materials with insulating foam, panel walls or double glazing for a pleasant working environment.
  • Safety and security: The booth must protect the staff inside and deter intruders. The material must be sturdy and able to carry security devices such as cameras, alarms and door locks. Tempered glass windows also improve security.
  • Appearance and architectural harmony: The booth should blend with its surroundings, such as urban areas, resorts, factories or office buildings. Aluminium-glass or wood-look composite not only look good but also give a professional impression consistent with the overall architecture.
  • Easy to build, transport and maintain: Flexibility in installation and maintenance matters when choosing material. Prefer light materials that are easy to transport and install, such as composite or aluminium. The material should also be easy to clean and little affected by weather to keep long-term maintenance costs low.
  • Investment cost: Choose according to your budget and purpose. Common materials such as composite and insulated sheet metal are moderately priced and suit short-term projects. Stainless steel and aluminium-glass cost more but offer better durability and appearance, suiting long-term projects with high image requirements.

Which material should a guard booth be made of?

Guard booths are usually placed outdoors. To stay durable and resist harsh weather, material choice is very important. By production material, guard booths fall into two main types: stainless steel booths and steel-frame booths clad with alloy panels.

Which material should a guard booth be made of

Stainless steel guard booths

This booth has a high-grade rust-free stainless steel frame, a non-slip aluminium floor and clear glass doors for easy observation. It comes with accessories such as power sockets, concealed wiring and switches. The width and height can be custom made.

The main advantage of this type is remarkable durability against weather and the environment. Stainless steel also improves the look of the interior.

However, stainless steel booths are usually fixed in place, which makes relocation difficult.

Stainless steel guard booth

Steel-frame guard booths clad with alloy

This booth is made of a stainless steel frame covered with aluminium alloy panels. Accessories include switches, sockets, a simple desk and more.

This model is compact, so it is easy to find a place for and easy to move. Designs and colors are varied, with different price levels to suit your taste and budget.

Despite these advantages, such booths are usually less luxurious than stainless steel ones and offer noticeably fewer amenities.

High-grade composite guard booths (FRP, WPC…)

Booths made of high-grade composite such as FRP (fiberglass-reinforced plastic) or WPC (wood-plastic composite) are valued for corrosion resistance, waterproofing and heat insulation. They are ideal for harsh environments such as coastal areas, industrial zones or high-humidity areas. The material is light, durable, termite-proof and easy to install and move. Composite can also be shaped flexibly to fit styles from modern to antique.

Aluminium frame with tempered glass guard booths

Aluminium-frame booths with tempered glass look modern and luxurious, suiting office buildings, upscale urban areas and shopping centers. The aluminium frame is light yet strong and does not rust, while tempered glass gives good visibility and high safety. The design also keeps the interior bright and professional, and makes it easy to add cameras, lights, air conditioning and more.

Soundproof galvanized steel guard booths

Galvanized steel is popular for its outstanding rigidity, load capacity and long life. With an inner soundproofing layer such as mineral wool or PU foam, the booth becomes a quiet workspace suited to noisy places such as construction sites, factories or toll stations. The galvanized coating also resists rust, is easy to maintain and performs steadily in harsh weather.

Soundproof galvanized steel guard booth

Galvanized or cold-rolled sheet guard booths

Booths made of galvanized or cold-rolled sheet have reasonable cost, fast construction and high durability. Cold-rolled sheet has an insulating layer that lowers interior temperature in the sun, while galvanized sheet resists rust in humid environments. This type suits industrial parks, factories, parking lots and projects that need to save cost while keeping basic functions.

Galvanized or cold-rolled sheet guard booth

Engineered wood or natural wood guard booths

For projects with high aesthetic requirements and a unified architecture, booths of engineered or natural wood are a refined choice for resorts, upscale retreats or villas. Natural wood gives a rustic, luxurious look, while properly treated engineered wood resists warping and termites. Wood needs regular maintenance to keep its durability and looks over time.

Engineered wood or natural wood guard booth

Suitable spaces for each type of guard booth

The right booth depends not only on material but also on the specifics of each space. Below are design suggestions for each type of location to get the best use and appearance.

Residential areas, apartment buildings, new urban areas

In modern residential and urban areas, a guard booth is not only the security staff workplace but also contributes to a professional, tidy landscape. Choose refined designs made of aluminium-glass, wood-look composite or powder-coated steel frames that serve their function and blend with the surrounding architecture.

Factories, industrial parks

In harsh environments such as factories or industrial parks, durability and load capacity come first. Booths here should be made of galvanized steel, insulated sheet metal or FRP composite, materials that resist corrosion and noise and withstand continuous operation. Also pay attention to sound insulation and ventilation to protect the health of the users.

Resorts and retreats

In upscale spaces such as resorts, a guard booth provides security and is also part of the landscape design. Booths should be made of natural wood, wood-plastic or wood-grain composite for a friendly, welcoming and luxurious feel. The design should match the overall architecture, blend with greenery and natural light, and can be customized for each area of the resort.

Schools and hospitals

In places with many people and special security needs, such as schools and hospitals, booths should use tempered glass for easy observation and quick response. Aluminium or galvanized steel frames are preferred for durability, safety, easy cleaning and suitability for public environments. Pay attention to the interior so staff on long shifts are comfortable.

Temporary or mobile sites

On temporary construction sites or at places that move often, booths should be made of light materials such as composite, galvanized sheet or aluminium frames for easy assembly and transport. The design should be simple and compact yet keep basic features such as a roof, ventilation and secure locks, and be easy to customize to the actual site.

Standard guard booth sizes

Once you have chosen the material, the next step is to determine the standard size. The right size optimizes usability and fits the installation space. Common guard booth sizes include: 1.2×1.2×2.4 m; 1.2×1.5×2.4 m; 1.5×1.5×2.4 m; 1.5×2.0x2.4 m; 2.0×2.0x2.4 m, and larger sizes on special order.

  • For large sites that need several guards, such as industrial parks, resorts, urban areas and schools, choose booths of 1.5×2.0 m or larger. These are roomy enough for 3 to 4 guards, with a fan, a desk or a small filing cabinet. Glass on several sides gives 360-degree observation for effective security.
  • For limited spaces such as parking entrances, small residential areas and side gates, choose smaller booths such as 1.2×1.2 m, 1.2×1.5 m or 1.5×1.5 m. They take little space and are easy to install on small sites, saving investment while keeping the basic functions for one guard on duty.
