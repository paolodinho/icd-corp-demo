ICD to take part in VIMEXPO 2020, the Vietnam Supporting Industry and Manufacturing Exhibition

Published22/10/2020
Reading time5 min read

VIMEXPO is Vietnam's first international exhibition on supporting industries and manufacturing, part of the supporting industry development program approved by the Prime Minister and organized under the guidance of the Ministry of Industry and Trade. VIMEXPO 2020 has the theme "CONNECTING FOR DEVELOPMENT".

  • Diversifying supporting industry products of the 6 target industry groups; presenting supporting industry achievements to domestic and international partners.
  • An effective connection program between suppliers and potential manufacturers.
  • An opportunity for dialogue between businesses and state agencies on supporting policies.
  • A starting point for Vietnamese manufacturers to strengthen joint ventures, cooperation and sustainable development, and participate effectively in global supply chains.

With 250 booths from 150 domestic and foreign companies, VIMEXPO 2020 expects over 18,000 visitors.
