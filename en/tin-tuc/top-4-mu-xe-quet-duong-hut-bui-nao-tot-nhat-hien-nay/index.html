Isuzu 5-cubic-meter street sweeper truck

The 5-cubic-meter street sweeper truck from the Isuzu brand is one of the most widely used vehicles for environmental cleaning. This model is equipped with a rotating brush system and small water spray nozzles. When the vehicle starts, the system automatically sprays water, and the rotating brushes continuously sweep to gather sand and dust.

Isuzu street sweeper truck with 5-cubic-meter capacity

Fiorentini S150 industrial street sweeper truck

S150 Fiorentini industrial street sweeper

Notes When Using the Street Sweeper with Vacuum

  • Only allow experienced operators to use the sweeper to ensure the safety of the user and other road users.
  • Operate the street sweeper according to the manufacturer’s specified technical guidelines.
  • Regularly empty the waste container to avoid affecting the equipment’s operation.
  • Clean and maintain the machine regularly to prevent dust from forming a thick layer.
  • Change oil periodically to ensure smooth machine operation. Note that the lubricant must be suitable for the machine.
