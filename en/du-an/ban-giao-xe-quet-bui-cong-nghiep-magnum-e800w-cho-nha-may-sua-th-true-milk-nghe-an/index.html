ICD was selected by TH Group to supply the Magnum E800W ride-on sweeper to the TH grain-processing plant in Nghia Dan, Nghe An Province, helping the plant meet environmental quality standards.

TH’s fresh-milk farm and processing system in Nghia Dan has been developed since October 2009 with total investment of more than USD 1.2 billion, among the largest dairy processing plants in Southeast Asia.
