]]> Hue Urban Environment and Public Works JSC - electric 3-wheel waste collection vehicles supplied by ICD https://paolodinho.github.io/icd-corp-demo/en/du-an/cong-ty-co-phan-moi-truong-va-cong-trinh-do-thi-hue-cung-cap-xe-thu-gom-rac-chay-dien-3-banh/ Tue, 02 Jun 2020 10:19:57 +0000 https://paolodinho.github.io/icd-corp-demo/du-an/cong-ty-co-phan-moi-truong-va-cong-trinh-do-thi-hue-cung-cap-xe-thu-gom-rac-chay-dien-3-banh/ Hue Urban Environment and Public Works JSC (HEPCO) inaugurated a smart monitoring and control centre and launched a pilot of household waste collection with electric vehicles in Hue City. ICD helped build and supply several electric waste-collection models for the programme.

Rollout plan

  • 2019 - 2020: 40 electric vehicles in the city centre and on sloped streets.
  • Phase 2 (2020 - 2022): 60 more vehicles in the remaining areas.

Vehicle features

  • Power of 1,000 - 2,000 W, design speed not exceeding 35 km/h.
  • Battery runs 8 - 10 hours continuously, matching collection hours on each route.
  • Headlights, turn signals, work-warning lights, a public-address speaker and a dedicated waste box.

According to HEPCO, electric vehicles reduce heavy and hazardous work for employees, raise sanitation quality and widen the collection area.
