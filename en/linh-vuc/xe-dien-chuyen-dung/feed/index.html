Advantages of special-purpose electric vehicles built by ICD

  • Easy to use and simple to operate.
  • Very low noise and no emissions inside plants and warehouses.
  • Solid construction, stable and safe operation.

Pictured: a 3-wheel ride-on tow tractor with a 48V - 1000W motor, built by ICD.

QDD03 high-speed mini tow tractor (imported)

  • 300 kg capacity; stand-on type with hand-lever steering.
  • Speed 12 km/h fully loaded and 14 km/h unloaded; gradeability 8% loaded and 16% unloaded.
  • 24V / 30Ah battery with charger; overall size 1,550 x 810 mm; manufacturer EP.
  • Compact and agile, with a forward/reverse button and a slow reverse speed for safety.
  • Suited to moving small loads in warehouses and patrolling production areas.
